Project Timeline

Our standard 6-week approach to deploying a custom show.

Weeks 1-2

Design & Simulation

Storyboarding, 3D path generation, and rendering a realistic simulation for client approval.

Weeks 3-4

Logistics & Airspace

Filing Part 107 waivers, NOTAMs, and conducting detailed site surveys at the launch location.

Week 5

Programming & QA

Uploading flight paths to the fleet, testing hardware redundancy, and verifying battery health.

Week 6

Rehearsal & Execution

On-site grid setup, closed rehearsal flight, and the final live performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if it rains?

Our drones can withstand light drizzle, but high winds (above 20mph) or heavy rain will require a weather hold. We always build weather contingency dates into our contracts.

How much space is needed for takeoff?

We require a secured launch footprint of at least 100x100 feet for a 200-drone show, completely clear of public access.

Can the drones fly over the audience?

Under current FAA regulations, we do not fly directly over unprotected persons. We design our flight paths to maintain a safe horizontal standoff distance while maximizing viewing angles.
