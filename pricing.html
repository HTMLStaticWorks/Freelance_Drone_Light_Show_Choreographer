Pricing FAQ

Are travel costs included?

The starting prices do not include travel and lodging for the flight crew or freight shipping for the drones. These costs are calculated based on your specific location during the quoting phase.

What is the payment schedule?

We typically require a 50% deposit to lock in the dates and begin the 3D design and FAA waiver process. The remaining 50% is due 7 days prior to the event.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes, we offer a 10% discount on the base package price for registered 501(c)(3) organizations for civic and community events.

What if weather cancels the show?

If weather grounds the fleet, we will attempt to fly on the pre-agreed weather contingency date at no additional cost. If both dates are rained out, a partial refund is provided minus sunk logistics costs.
