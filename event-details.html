The Evolution of RTK GPS in Swarm Robotics

David Chen Oct 12, 2026 5 min read

In the early days of drone light shows, standard GPS provided positioning accuracy within a few meters. Today, Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GPS has reduced that margin of error to mere centimeters.

Why Accuracy Matters

When you are flying 1,000 drones in close proximity, a gust of wind or a momentary GPS glitch could lead to a cascading collision. By utilizing a ground-based RTK base station that communicates corrections to the fleet in real-time, we maintain an absolute lock on each drone's position.

Our proprietary mobile RTK base station deployed at a launch site.

Unlocking Complex Geometries

This pinpoint accuracy isn't just about safety; it's about art. RTK allows us to decrease the spacing between drones. Instead of a 3-meter safety bubble, we can safely operate with a 1.5-meter bubble. This denser spacing allows for much higher resolution "pixels" in the sky, making complex 3D logos and readable text possible.

"RTK didn't just make drone shows safer; it fundamentally upgraded the resolution of the sky."

As we look to the future, the integration of visual odometry alongside RTK will allow for indoor swarms and flights in GPS-denied environments like deep urban canyons.
