About Us

Our Story

From aerospace engineers to sky artists, we founded AeroGlow to merge technological precision with emotional storytelling.

Pioneering Aerial Robotics

AeroGlow was established in 2021 by a collective of former aerospace engineers and 3D animators. We recognized a gap in the entertainment industry: the sky was an underutilized canvas.

What started with a fleet of 50 customized drones has now expanded to a proprietary arsenal of over 5,000 aircraft, capable of executing complex geometries, brand logos, and dynamic storytelling sequences synchronized to music and pyrotechnics.

Our Mission

To revolutionize live entertainment by providing accessible, sustainable, and awe-inspiring drone choreography that elevates how humanity celebrates.

Our Vision

To become the global standard for nighttime aerial displays, eventually replacing traditional pyrotechnics with zero-emission, infinitely reusable smart drone swarms.
